Secondary Bibliography

1. Sears, Andrew; Jacko, Julie A. (2007).
   The Human–Computer Interaction Handbook: Fundamentals, Evolving Technologies and Emerging Applications.
   CRC Press.
   – Includes a historical overview of early HCI systems, citing Sketchpad as a foundational example of graphical interaction.

2. Armstrong, Helen (ed.) (2016).
   Digital Design Theory: Readings from the Field.
   Princeton Architectural Press.
   – Discusses Sketchpad in the context of digital design tools, framing it as a key precedent for computational design.

3. Yares, Evan (2013).
   “50 Years of CAD.” Design World, February 2013, pp. 66–71.
   – Retrospective article for the 50th anniversary of Sketchpad, situating it in the lineage of CAD and 3D modeling.

4. Müller-Prove, Matthias.
   “Graphical User Interface of Sketchpad.”
   – Online essay analyzing Sketchpad’s GUI, interaction patterns, and its influence on later GUI designs.

5. Kay, Alan (various talks and essays, e.g., 2007, 2024).
   – Reflective commentary on Sketchpad’s influence on Smalltalk, object-oriented programming, and modern GUI concepts.

6. Computer History Museum.
   “Sketchpad and the History of Computer-Aided Design.”
   – Curatorial texts and catalog entries describing Sketchpad’s role in the development of CAD and interactive graphics.

7. American Machinist (1998).
   “The CAD/CAM Hall of Fame.”
   – Trade article recognizing Sutherland’s Sketchpad as a breakthrough in 3D modeling and CAD/CAM history.
